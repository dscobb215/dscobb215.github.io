Blueberry Pie

Serves 8-10

Prep Time: 1 Hour

Intro/Backround Of The Recipe

I chose this blueberry pie recipe because it has to be one of the greatest tasting pies I've ever had. This recipe was written by my grandmother on my mother's side and it was a staple of growing up at her house. It's a classic blueberry pie with a couple personal twists. The main being her secret ingredient, gin. Like she always says It's the secret ingredient that makes it special.

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Blueberries 4 Cups
Sugar 1 Cup
Flour 1/4 Cup
Lemon Zest (or Lemon Juice) 1 Teaspoon (or 2 Teaspoons)
Gin 1 Tablespoon
Butter 2 Tablespoons
Pie Crusts 2 (Recipe of your choosing)

Instructions

  1. Toss berries with sugar and flower
  2. Line pie dish with selected pie crust
  3. Pour berries evenly over pie crust
  4. Sprinkle zest and gin evenly over berries
  5. Carefully lay second pie crust over berries, fold
    overhanging dough under the overhang
    from the bottom crust and crimp and cut small
    holes for ventilation
  7. Bake at 425oF for 35-45 minutes
  8. Slice, serve, and enjoy!

Tips And Tricks

  • Sprinkle a little granulated sugar on the top of the pie in the last
    10 minutes of cooking for a little extra sweetness.
  • If you want to cut your prep time, use store bought dough instead
    of making it yourself!
