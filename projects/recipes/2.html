Blueberry Pie

Backround Of The Recipe

This recipe was written by my grandmother on my father's side. It's a pumpkin roll for both christmas and the
fall.

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Large Eggs 3
Granulated Sugar 1 Cup
Pumpkin Puree 2/3 Cup
Lemon Juice 1 Teaspoon
Flour 3/4 Cup
Baking Powder 1 Teaspoon
Cinnamon 2 Teaspoons
Ginger 1 Teaspoon
Nutmeg 1/2 Teaspoon
Salt 1/2 Teaspoon

Directions/Steps

  1. Beat all 3 eggs on high with mixer
  2. Gradually beat in granulated sugar
  3. Pour berries evenly over pie crust
  4. Springkle zest evenly, and gently pour gin over berries
  5. Close top of pie with strips of pie crust, weave with few gaps
    for ventilation
  6. Dot top lightly with butter
  7. Bake at 425oF for 35-45 minutes
  8. Slice, serve, and enjoy!
