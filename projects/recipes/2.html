Pumpkin Roll

Serves 6-8

Prep Time: 2 Hours

Intro/Backround Of The Recipe

This recipe was written by my grandmother on my fathers side. Its a classic pumpkin roll for both christmas and the fall. Growing up we always had one of these cakes around christmas and thanksgiving. My dad grew up with his grandmother making this cake and he said you always knew Chrsistmas was right around the corner when you smelled cinnamon and vanilla coming from the kitchen.It was a staple of Friday Night Coffee in my Dad's household.

Ingredients

For The Cake

Ingredient Amount
Large Eggs 3
Granulated Sugar 1 Cup
Pumpkin Spice 2/3 Cup
Lemon Juice 1 Teaspoon
Flour 3/4 Cup
Baking Powder 1 Teaspoon
Cinnamon 2 Teaspoons
Ginger 1 Teaspoon
Nutmeg 1/2 Teaspoon
Salt 1/2 Teaspoon
Walnuts 1 Cup
Powdered Sugar 1/2 Cup Powdered Sugar

For The Filling

Ingredient Amount
Powdered Sugar 1 cup
Cream Cheese 2–3 oz
Butter 4 tablespoons
Vanilla 1 tablespoon

Directions/Steps

  1. Beat all 3 eggs on high with mixer.
  2. Gradually beat in granulated sugar, pumpkin spice, and lemon juice.
  3. Sift together in a seperate bowl flour, baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and salt.
  4. Fold mixture into the egg/pumpkin mixture.
  5. Spread batter into greased and floured 15x10x1 inch pan.
  6. Top with chopped walnuts.
  7. Bake at 375o F for 15 minutes.
  8. Turn out onto a dishtowel sprinkled with powdered sugar.
  9. Starting at the narrow end, roll towel and cake together
  10. Cool for 1 hour
  11. Make filling by combining powdered sugar, cream cheese, butter, vanilla and beat until smooth.
  12. Once the cake is cool, unroll, remove dish towel, and spread the filling over the top of the cake.
  13. Reroll cake and chill
  14. Slice, serve, and enjoy!
